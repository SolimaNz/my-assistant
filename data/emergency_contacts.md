# Emergency Contacts and Essential Services in Egypt

## Emergency Numbers
- **Police:** 122
- **Ambulance:** 123
- **Fire Department:** 180
- **Tourist Police:** 126 (speak English, specifically for tourist issues)
- **Traffic Police:** 128
- **Electricity Emergency:** 121
- **Natural Gas Emergency:** 129
- **Coast Guard / Maritime Rescue:** 3805-0521

Always call Tourist Police (126) first for tourist-related issues — they're trained to handle language barriers and tourist-specific problems.

## Hospitals with English-Speaking Staff

- **As-Salam International Hospital (Maadi):** Corniche El Nil, Maadi. Tel: +20 2 2524 0250. 24/7 emergency. Accepts international insurance.
- **Dar Al Fouad Hospital (6th of October):** 26th of July Corridor, 6th of October City. Tel: +20 2 3835 6030. Major trauma center.
- **Cairo Cure Hospital (New Cairo):** 5th Settlement. Tel: +20 2 2537 3333. Modern facility.
- **Al Mokattam Military Hospital (Armed Forces):** High-quality care, sometimes accessible to foreigners in emergencies.

### Alexandria
- **Alexandria University Hospital:** Champolion Street. Tel: +20 3 486 2091.
- **German Hospital Alexandria:** Tel: +20 3 584 6364.

### Hurghada / Red Sea
- **El Gouna Hospital:** Well-equipped, English-speaking staff. Tel: +20 65 358 0012.
- **Hurghada General Hospital:** For emergencies. Quality varies.

### Sharm El Sheikh
- **Sharm International Hospital:** Tel: +20 69 366 0893. English-speaking staff, handles diving-related injuries and decompression sickness.
- **Hyperbaric Medical Center:** For diving emergencies specifically.

## Pharmacies
- **El Ezaby Pharmacy:** Largest chain in Egypt. Found in almost every neighborhood. Open late, many branches 24/7. Staff usually speak some English.
- **Seif Pharmacy:** Another large chain with wide coverage.
- Most pharmacies in Egypt sell medications over the counter that require prescriptions in other countries.
- Common medications are very affordable (antibiotics, pain relief, allergy meds).

## Lost Passport Procedure
1. File a police report at the nearest police station. Get a stamped copy (محضر فقدان).
2. Contact your embassy immediately. They can issue an emergency travel document.
3. Bring: police report, 2 passport photos, any copy/photo of your old passport, and the embassy fee (varies by country, typically $100–200 USD or equivalent).
4. Processing time: same-day to 3 business days for emergency documents.
5. You'll also need to visit the Egyptian Immigration Office (Mugamma building, Tahrir Square) if your visa was in the lost passport.

## Lost or Stolen Phone
1. File a police report (محضر سرقة or محضر فقدان).
2. Note your IMEI number (dial *#06# before you lose it — save it separately).
3. Contact your carrier to block the SIM.
4. Report to NTRA (National Telecom Regulatory Authority) to blacklist the IMEI.

## Travel Insurance
- Highly recommended for all visitors.
- Ensure it covers: medical evacuation, emergency medical treatment, and trip cancellation.
- Diving-specific insurance recommended for Red Sea trips (DAN or similar).
- Most Egyptian hospitals require upfront payment — insurance reimburses later.
