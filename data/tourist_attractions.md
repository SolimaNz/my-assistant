# Top Tourist Attractions in Egypt — Practical Visitor Information

> **IMPORTANT (2026):** Most major tourist sites in Egypt now require payment by **credit or debit card** (Visa/Mastercard). Cash is no longer accepted at many ticket counters. Always carry a card. Tickets can also be booked online at egymonuments.com.

## Cairo & Giza

### Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx
- **Location:** Al Haram, Giza Governorate. GPS: 29.9792, 31.1342
- **Hours:** 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM (winter), 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM (summer)
- **Entry Fee:** 700 EGP general area (includes Sphinx enclosure and panoramic viewpoints), 350 EGP student. Going inside the Great Pyramid of Khufu: 1,500 EGP extra. Pyramid of Menkaure interior: 280 EGP extra.
- **Tips:** Go early morning (7–8 AM) to avoid crowds and heat. The south gate entrance is less crowded than the north. Licensed camel rides: negotiate 200–300 EGP for 30 minutes. The Sound & Light Show runs nightly — book online.
- **Getting There:** Uber from Downtown Cairo ~150–250 EGP. Metro Line 2 to Giza station, then taxi.
- **Best Time:** October–March (cooler weather). Friday mornings are busiest.

### The Egyptian Museum (Tahrir Square)
- **Location:** Tahrir Square, Downtown Cairo. GPS: 30.0478, 31.2336
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily
- **Entry Fee:** 550 EGP general, 275 EGP student.
- **Tips:** Allow 2–3 hours minimum. Hire a guide at the entrance (~300–500 EGP) — the museum has minimal English signage. Photography allowed without flash.
- **Note:** Many exhibits are being moved to the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM).

### Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM)
- **Location:** Near the Pyramids of Giza, Al Remaya Square. GPS: 29.9953, 31.1163
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (check current schedule as it's newly opened)
- **Entry Fee:** 1,450 EGP general, 730 EGP student/youth (13–25). Tickets MUST be booked online at visit-gem.com — no on-site ticket sales for foreign visitors.
- **Tips:** The largest archaeological museum in the world. Tutankhamun's complete collection is here. Allocate at least 4–5 hours. Combined Pyramids + GEM day trip is ideal.

### Islamic Cairo (Old Cairo / Fatimid Cairo)
- **Key Sites:** Al-Azhar Mosque, Khan El-Khalili bazaar, Al-Muizz Street, Bab Zuweila, Al-Hakim Mosque, Sultan Hassan Mosque, Al-Rifai Mosque.
- **Location:** El-Gamaleya, Islamic Cairo. GPS: 30.0459, 31.2623
- **Hours:** Mosques open roughly 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM for tourists (closed during prayer times). Khan El-Khalili shops: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
- **Entry Fee:** Most mosques are free. Some charge 20–60 EGP.
- **Tips:** Wear modest clothing. Walk Al-Muizz Street end to end — it's one of the oldest streets in the world. Best visited late afternoon when the light is beautiful and shops are lively. Bargain hard at Khan El-Khalili.

### Coptic Cairo
- **Key Sites:** Hanging Church (Al-Mu'allaqa), Church of St. Sergius, Ben Ezra Synagogue, Coptic Museum.
- **Location:** Mar Girgis, Old Cairo. GPS: 30.0058, 31.2305
- **Metro:** Mar Girgis station (Line 1) — right at the entrance.
- **Hours:** 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM. Churches open for visits outside of service times.
- **Entry Fee:** Churches are free. Coptic Museum: 280 EGP, 140 EGP student.
- **Tips:** Compact area, walkable in 2–3 hours. Combine with a visit to the Amr Ibn Al-As Mosque nearby.

### Cairo Citadel (Salah El-Din Citadel)
- **Location:** Al Abageyah, Cairo. GPS: 30.0288, 31.2599
- **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Entry Fee:** 550 EGP general, 275 EGP student. Includes access to mosques and museums within the complex.
- **Highlights:** Muhammad Ali Mosque (the "Alabaster Mosque"), panoramic views of Cairo, military museums.
- **Tips:** Go in late afternoon for the best photos and sunset views over the city.

## Luxor

### Karnak Temple
- **Location:** East Bank, Luxor. GPS: 25.7188, 32.6573
- **Hours:** 6:00 AM – 5:30 PM
- **Entry Fee:** 600 EGP general, 300 EGP student.
- **Tips:** Largest ancient religious site in the world. Go at opening (6 AM) to beat tour groups. The Sound & Light Show at night is worth seeing. Allow 2–3 hours.

### Valley of the Kings
- **Location:** West Bank, Luxor. GPS: 25.7402, 32.6014
- **Hours:** 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Entry Fee:** 750 EGP general, 375 EGP student (includes 3 standard tombs). Tomb of Tutankhamun and Tomb of Seti I require separate additional tickets.
- **Tips:** No photography inside tombs (strictly enforced). Arrive early — tombs get extremely hot and crowded. Bring water. West Bank sites can be reached by ferry from East Bank (~5 EGP for locals' ferry, ~25 EGP tourist ferry).

### Luxor Temple
- **Location:** East Bank, Corniche. GPS: 25.6996, 32.6391
- **Hours:** 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM
- **Entry Fee:** 500 EGP general, 250 EGP student.
- **Tips:** Visit at night — the illuminated temple is spectacular. Walking distance from most East Bank hotels.

### Hatshepsut Temple (Deir el-Bahari)
- **Location:** West Bank, Luxor. GPS: 25.7381, 32.6066
- **Hours:** 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Entry Fee:** 360 EGP general, 180 EGP student.
- **Tips:** One of the most impressive temples in Egypt. Combine with Valley of the Kings in a single West Bank day trip.

## Aswan

### Philae Temple
- **Location:** Agilkia Island, Aswan. GPS: 24.0263, 32.8842
- **Hours:** 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM (winter), 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM (summer)
- **Entry Fee:** 550 EGP general, 275 EGP student. Boat ride to the island is negotiated separately at the pier.
- **Tips:** Beautiful setting on an island. The boat ride is part of the experience. Sound & Light Show available at night.

### Abu Simbel
- **Location:** Abu Simbel, 280 km south of Aswan. GPS: 22.3360, 31.6256
- **Hours:** 5:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- **Entry Fee:** 822 EGP general, 445 EGP student. During Sun Festival dates (Feb 22 & Oct 22): 1,272 EGP adult, 670 EGP student.
- **Getting There:** 3-hour drive from Aswan (convoy departs at 4:00 AM), or 45-minute flight (EgyptAir).
- **Tips:** One of Egypt's most iconic sites. The sun alignment phenomenon happens on February 22 and October 22. Book a Aswan–Abu Simbel day trip through your hotel.

## Red Sea

### Hurghada
- Popular for snorkeling, diving, and beach resorts. Giftun Island day trips: ~400–600 EGP including boat, lunch, and snorkeling gear.
- Best diving sites: Giftun Islands, Abu Ramada, Shaab El Erg (dolphin reef).

### Sharm El Sheikh
- World-class diving: Ras Mohammed National Park (entry 75 EGP + park fees), Tiran Island, Blue Hole (Dahab — for experienced divers only).
- Naama Bay: main tourist strip with restaurants, shops, nightlife.
- Old Market (Sharm El Maya): good for souvenirs and local food.

## Alexandria

### Bibliotheca Alexandrina
- **Location:** Corniche, Chatby, Alexandria. GPS: 31.2089, 29.9092
- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (closed Fridays)
- **Entry Fee:** 70 EGP
- **Tips:** Modern library built near the site of the ancient Library of Alexandria. Beautiful architecture. Contains several museums and galleries inside.

### Qaitbay Citadel
- **Location:** Eastern Harbor tip, Alexandria. GPS: 31.2139, 29.8855
- **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Entry Fee:** 60 EGP
- **Tips:** Built on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria (one of the Seven Wonders). Great sea views.
