# Cairo Transportation Guide

## Cairo Metro
The Cairo Metro has 3 operational lines:
- **Line 1 (Helwan–New El-Marg):** Oldest line, runs north-south through Downtown. Key stops: Sadat (Tahrir Square), Mar Girgis (Coptic Cairo), Maadi, Helwan.
- **Line 2 (Shubra–El-Mounib):** Crosses the Nile twice. Key stops: Shubra El-Kheima, Attaba (transfer to Line 1), Opera, Cairo University, Giza, El-Mounib.
- **Line 3 (Adly Mansour–Rod El-Farag Corridor):** Newest line, serves the airport area and northeast Cairo. Key stops: Cairo Airport, Heliopolis, Attaba (transfer), Kit Kat.

### Metro Pricing (updated March 2026)
- Up to 9 stations: 10 EGP
- Up to 16 stations: 12 EGP
- Up to 23 stations: 15 EGP
- Over 23 stations: 20 EGP
- Tokens are purchased at station booths or from vending machines.
- Subscription systems available for regular commuters (students, employees) at discounted rates.
- Women-only cars available (middle cars), especially recommended during rush hours.

### Metro Operating Hours
- First train: 5:15 AM
- Last train: 12:00 AM (midnight)
- During Ramadan: extended to 1:00 AM
- Frequency: 3–5 minutes during rush hour, 8–10 minutes off-peak.

## Taxis
### White Taxis (Metered)
- Start fare: approximately 12–15 EGP
- Per kilometer: approximately 5–7 EGP
- Always insist on the meter. If the driver refuses, get another taxi.
- Tip: Round up or add 5–10 EGP.

### Black Taxis (Older Fleet)
- No meter — negotiate price before getting in.
- Generally cheaper but less comfortable.
- Being phased out gradually.

## Ride-Hailing Apps
- **Uber:** Available across Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, and some other cities. Prices shown upfront. Credit card or cash accepted. Most reliable option for tourists.
- **Careem (by Uber):** Similar coverage. Has a "Careem Bus" option for shared rides at lower cost.
- **InDriver:** Budget option where you bid your price. Popular with locals.

### Typical Ride-Hailing Prices (Cairo, 2026)
- Airport to Downtown Cairo: 350–500 EGP (Uber), 30–45 minutes
- Downtown to Pyramids of Giza: 150–250 EGP, 30–50 minutes
- Maadi to Zamalek: 100–180 EGP, 20–40 minutes
- Heliopolis to Downtown: 80–150 EGP, 20–35 minutes
- Prices are dynamic — surge pricing applies during peak hours (10 PM–2 AM, 6–11 AM).

## Microbuses
- Cheapest option (10–15 EGP for most routes).
- No fixed stops — wave one down, tell them your destination, and pay when you get off.
- Routes are not officially mapped. Ask locals which microbus goes where.
- Crowded and fast. Not recommended for first-time visitors unless with a local.

## Bus Rapid Transit (BRT)
- Electric air-conditioned buses on dedicated lanes along the Cairo Ring Road.
- Phase I operational: 35 km with 14 stations.
- Distance-based fare: up to 4 stops: 5 EGP, up to 9 stops: 10 EGP, 9+ stops: 15 EGP.
- Integrates with Metro, Light Rail Transit (LRT), and future monorail via upcoming unified transport card.
- Google Maps sometimes shows BRT routes.

## Cairo Airport Transportation
- **Terminal 1:** Older terminal, some budget airlines.
- **Terminal 2:** Main international terminal (EgyptAir hub).
- **Terminal 3:** Newest, connected to Metro Line 3.
- Metro from Airport to Downtown: ~15–20 EGP (Line 3), takes about 45–60 minutes.
- Uber/Careem from Airport: 350–500 EGP to Downtown (higher during surge hours).
- Official airport taxi booth (prepaid): ~400–550 EGP to Downtown.
- Avoid unlicensed taxi drivers inside the terminal — walk outside or use apps.

## Tips for Getting Around Cairo
- Rush hours: 8–10 AM and 4–7 PM. Avoid driving during these times.
- Google Maps works well for navigation and traffic estimation.
- The Nile bridges get extremely congested — plan crossings outside rush hour.
- Walking in Downtown Cairo is often faster than driving during peak times.
- Many one-way streets in Downtown — taxi/Uber routes may seem roundabout.
